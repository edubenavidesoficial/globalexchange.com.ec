BELGIUM

Tuition fees in Belgium range around €1,000-1,500 depending on the degree. For foreigners who are members of the European Union, the two university systems apply unlike Germany or France, and additional costs for not having contributed to income tax. Culturally and linguistically the kingdom of Belgium is divided into 2 The Francophone university system and the Flemish university system. Francophone universities and schools are essentially located in the Brussels region. The best known universities are the Free University of Brussels (ULB) and the Catholic University of Louvain (UCL). Flemish universities and schools are located in the cities of Antwerp, Ghent and Brussels.
The main ones are the Universiteit Antwerpen (UA) and the Universiteit Gent (UGent).

  • Universiteit Antwerpen (UA)
  • Universidad Católica de Louvain (UCL).

Los dos sistemas educativos se dividen entre universidades públicas y universidades católicas (privadas). Si te planteas venir a estudiar a las universidades o escuelas belgas, te aconsejamos empezar con 9 meses de antelación con el fin de respetar todas las fechas límites y no quedarte fuera por falta de tiempo.

    TRAINING

    Training is an educational measure in which knowledge and skills are imparted or developed, the completion of which, after training, people can carry out a specific activity.
    In addition to school education, it includes vocational training and studies at a higher education institution (university or university of applied sciences).
    In ausbildung, you attend vocational school and learn in the company.
    The training lasts 3 years and after that a recognized training certificate is obtained.

    DUAL STUDY

    The basic component of a dual study program is the combination of practical work in a company and theoretical classes in a university or cooperative training centre. These two areas of work (practical and theoretical) alternate with each other with a normally regular rhythm (time models).
    This means, for example, that you complete the training in the company for 3 months and then study for another 3 months.
